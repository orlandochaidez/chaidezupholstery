Coast route questions

How long will my pieces be gone?

About a week — picked up Saturday, delivered back the following Saturday.

Where is the work done?

In our full upholstery shop in Bakersfield — over 35 years, all our equipment and materials under one roof. That's how route pricing stays honest: you're sharing one truck, not paying for a mobile setup.

What does pickup and delivery cost?

It's built into the route price. One truck serves every stop, so nobody pays for a special trip.

Is there a minimum job?

Route pricing is built for batches — a booth section, a set of boat seats, a room of chairs. One or two small pieces are welcome when the truck has room; the office will tell you straight.

How do deposits work?

50% when we pick up, the rest when your pieces are back in place. No deposit, no pickup — that keeps the route fair for everyone on it.

How do I get started?

Call the shop and ask about the Central Coast route, or send photos on Instagram @chaidezupholstery. We'll price the whole list free.
